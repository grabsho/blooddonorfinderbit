🩸 Why Donate?

One donation can save up to 3 lives. Blood cannot be manufactured — it only comes from donors.

📋 Who Can Donate?

Age 18–65, weight ≥45kg, healthy, no recent infections. Men: every 3 months. Women: every 4 months.

⏱️ Process

Registration → Health check → Donation (10 min) → Refreshments → Rest. Total: ~30 minutes.
