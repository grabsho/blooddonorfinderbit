Care, safety and responsibility

Blood Donor Finder is a college project designed to help people find potential blood donors using the available directory.

A directory match does not guarantee donor availability, blood compatibility, or medical eligibility. Always confirm details directly and follow guidance from a qualified healthcare professional or blood bank.

For a life-threatening emergency, contact local emergency services or the nearest appropriate medical facility immediately. Do not wait for an online response.
